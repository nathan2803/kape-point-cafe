Our Roots & Purpose

Brewing with Intention, Honoring Our Growers

Kape Point Cafe began with a simple purpose: celebrating local coffee culture by bridging mountain farmers directly with urban coffee enthusiasts.

We collaborate with smallholder coffee farming communities across Benguet, Sagada, and Bukidnon. Every bean is carefully harvested at peak ripeness, sun-dried, and roasted in small weekly batches right here in our roastery.

✓
Direct-Trade Partnerships: Fair compensation directly supporting coffee farming families.
✓
Zero Waste Coffee Ground Upcycling: Organic coffee grounds repurposed as community garden compost.
✓
Welcoming Creative Space: High-speed optical Wi-Fi, ample power outlets, and naturally lit seating.
