Brewing with Intention, Honoring Local Roots

Kape Point Cafe was founded in Lucena City to celebrate Philippine highland coffee culture and create a space where neighbors, students, and remote creators can pause and connect.

Rather than relying on imported industrial blends, we roast single-origin beans in small weekly batches. We buy directly from grower cooperatives at prices well above commodity rates, ensuring sustainable livelihoods for farming families.

Direct Highland Trade

Directly sourcing from Benguet, Mountain Province, and Bukidnon farmers with full transparent traceability.

Sustainable Roastery Practice

Spent grounds are donated to local Lucena urban gardeners, and patrons bringing reusable cups receive ₱15 off.

Welcoming Work Sanctuary

Equipped with reliable optical fiber Wi-Fi, accessible power outlets at every booth, and comfortable wooden seating.

The Kape Point Craft

A disciplined approach to green bean sourcing, temperature-calibrated brewing, and daily sourdough baking.

Micro-Batch Roasting

Roasted in 5kg micro-batches to develop distinct origin flavors: floral bergamot, dark cacao, and brown sugar sweetness without harsh bitterness.

Calibrated Extraction

Every shot is dialed in each morning using digital TDS refractometry and precise 9-bar pressure curves for smooth, repeatable cup clarity.

Quiet Work Space

A balanced ambient environment designed for productivity, study sessions, reading, and thoughtful conversations without loud distractions.
